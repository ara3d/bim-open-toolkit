Volume is the enclosed mesh volume from view3d.measures, summed per element; Height is the element's vertical extent from its instance bounds. Storey is the nearest storey above an element in StoreyOfElement (smallest Depth), so each element sits on one storey. A class's median is the volume of its middle row by volume (table.window); Fold is how many times larger or smaller than that median an element is, never below 1. An element is an outlier when Fold is 10 or more in a class of at least 5 elements; spaces and openings are left out.
