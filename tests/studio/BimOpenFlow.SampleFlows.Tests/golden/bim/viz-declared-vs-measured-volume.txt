about = view.note@1(text: "Model auditor or quantity surveyor: do the volumes the model declares agree with its geometry? Declared is the Revit Volume parameter (PSet_Revit_Dimensions, m3) per element from the DuckDB export; Measured is the enclosed mesh volume from view3d.measures, summed over an element's meshes, exact only for closed meshes. Both are summed per class; a class's Declared total covers only the elements that declare one (WithDeclared against Elements in the table), and a class with none keeps it missing: doors, windows, furniture, stairs and railings declare no volume here, and so does the roof's slab. Gap_pct is measured minus declared as a percentage of declared. The grouped chart draws the two totals side by side per class that declares a volume, one colour per series; the second chart draws the gap, one colour per class. A large gap is a question for the model, not an error: the Duplex carries its roof twice (IfcRoof and an IfcSlab with the same name). The numbers are in samples/viz-analyses/README.md.");
