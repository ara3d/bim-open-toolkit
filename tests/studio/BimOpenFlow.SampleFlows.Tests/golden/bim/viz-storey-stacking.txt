Role: a BIM manager walking a client through how the Duplex is built up. Question: how many elements sit on each storey, and of which classes, from the foundation to the roof?
How to read it: 'chart' has one bar per storey, ordered by elevation, coloured on the viridis scale ('scale') the 3D view ('colored') uses to paint every element by its storey's elevation: purple is the foundation, yellow the roof. 'byClass' splits each storey into one series per element class (stacked in specs/viz-storey-stacking.vl.json).
How it counts: an element's storey is the nearest IfcBuildingStorey that contains it ('located'), so furniture counts on the storey of its room and stair parts on the storey of their stair. Spaces are left out ('physical'). The warning on 'withStorey' counts the entities no storey contains (types, openings, the project and site); they belong to no floor. Classes merge IFC names: Wall is IfcWall plus IfcWallStandardCase, Stair is IfcStair plus IfcStairFlight ('classes').
The numbers for the Duplex are in samples/viz-analyses/README.md.
""");
