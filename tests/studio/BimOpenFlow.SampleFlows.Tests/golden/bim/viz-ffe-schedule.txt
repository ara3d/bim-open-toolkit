One row per IfcFurnishingElement, its Revit family read from the first part of the name (M_Base Cabinet-Double Door & 2 Drawer:1000mm:1000mm, with the metric prefix M_ dropped) and its size from the second; the storey is the nearest IfcBuildingStorey that contains it. The chart ranks the families by count as horizontal bars in the single accent colour: colour would say nothing a rank does not, so colorBy is none. The table pivots the same items by storey, one column per storey, with the total. The counts for the Duplex are in samples/viz-analyses/README.md.");

items = duck.query@1(path: database.source, sql: "SELECT e.GlobalId, e.Name, regexp_replace(split_part(e.Name, ':', 1), '^M_', '') AS Family, split_part(e.Name, ':', 2) AS Size, s.Storey, t.Value AS Tag FROM EntityText e LEFT JOIN (SELECT EntityIndex, arg_min(StoreyName, Depth) AS Storey FROM StoreyOfElement GROUP BY EntityIndex) s ON s.EntityIndex = e.EntityIndex LEFT JOIN ParameterText t ON t.EntityIndex = e.EntityIndex AND t.Name = 'Ifc:Tag' WHERE e.Category = 'IFCFURNISHINGELEMENT' ORDER BY e.GlobalId");
