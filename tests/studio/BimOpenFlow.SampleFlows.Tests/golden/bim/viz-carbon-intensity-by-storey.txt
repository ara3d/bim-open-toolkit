Role: a sustainability lead. Question: how much embodied carbon (A1-A3) does each storey carry per square metre of floor, so storeys of different size compare fairly?
How it counts: carbon is summed per storey from nrc_analytics_elements.csv (synthetic); floor area is the sum of the IfcSpace areas on that storey (PSet_Revit_Dimensions Area, net room area, m2) from the enriched Duplex; the ratio is kgCO2e/m2. A storey with no spaces, such as T/FDN (the foundation), keeps a missing intensity, never zero: grey in 3D and 'no floor area' in the Vega-Lite figure. Roof's area is one space named Roof, the attic volume Revit modelled as a room, so its intensity measures the attic, not a living floor. ElementsWithCarbon counts the elements that have a carbon value; the IFCROOF has none.
How to read it: 'chart' ranks the storeys by intensity on one viridis scale ('scale'); the 3D view ('colored') tints every element with its storey's intensity on the same scale. 'buildingTable' sums every storey's carbon, the foundation included, and separately the carbon of only the storeys that have spaces; its intensity divides that second sum by their floor area, so the foundation's carbon is not spread over an area it does not have.
The numbers for the Duplex are in samples/viz-analyses/README.md.
""");
