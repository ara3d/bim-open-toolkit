## Findings
- Every result is bit-identical: each Once function or check makes the same calls on the same arguments with the repeats removed, and the agreement laws compare exactly (NaN equal to NaN).
- `Law_Perf_PcurveToCurveGapOnceAgrees` runs on the two GeometrySet witnesses, whose curves are not surface curves, so it is vacuous there; `Law_Perf_PcurveGapCheckOnceAgrees` covers the model witnesses' curves under the check plan.
- `Law_RigidMotionKeepsCoedgeSense` drew random coedge sites, almost all out of range on the witnesses (faces from -8 to 8); the restated law checks every site of every face, and passes.
- The assembly laws' baseline `pass` is mostly vacuous: their 25 generated face-mesh arrays are refused by `CanAssemble`. Over `AssembledWitness` they hold on two accepted boxes.
- The STEP claims over a `Part21File` cannot run under the law runner (`DecodeRaw` is a host obligation), so 16 of the 22 STEP claims are shown equal to their laws only by transcription; the six over a raw model are shown by `Law_Perf_RawStepClaimsAgree`. A `Part21File` witness with a decode (TKT-312's C# decoder) would let a law compare all 22.
- The original face-meshing self-tests are too slow to compare on all 42 triangulated witness faces inside 2 s compiled (29 s for the triangulation group alone), which is the cost this chunk removes; the agreement laws compare on the unit box, and on the cylinder where the original fits.
- Not done in this chunk, same pattern: `Check_EveryPointIsACorner` still scans every element per point (a marks table would make it linear), `Check_InteriorSideIsShared`'s `SideCount` scans every element per side, and `Check_PcurveAgreesWithProjection` measures every edge of the model per face (only the face's edges can fail).

## Lessons
- A per-sample lambda that passes `m.RegionOf(...)` or `f.DecodeBrep` as an argument rebuilds it per sample: the C# backend memoizes nothing. Bind it in a block body before the fold.
- An agreement law against a slow original costs what the original costs; pick witnesses the original finishes on, and measure the replacement alone with a probe law in the scratchpad.
