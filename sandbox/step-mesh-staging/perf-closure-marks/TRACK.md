## Contract requests
- Sortedness of `GeometrySet.Closures` becomes part of the arena's contract: `IsWellFormed(g)` gains `&& g.ClosuresAreSorted`, and `geometry-set.types.plato`'s comment on `Closures` says "in ClosureMarks's order (MarkRank, then the index)". Why: a lookup that must give the scan's answer on an unsorted list has to look at every mark, so the "fall back to the scan when the marks are not sorted" the dispatch asked for costs O(K) per call to decide, the cost this chunk removes. No arena in the library is unsorted today: layer 03's `ClosureMarks` emits the order, `WithClosures`, `WithCurve`, `WithSurface`, `ScaledGeometry`, `Moved` and the termination witnesses copy the marks unchanged, and the only hand-built marks (`bspline-inverse.laws.plato` line 53) are one mark. `Law_Perf_ClosureMarksAreSorted` checks every model, decoded and arena witness; `Law_Perf_ClosureMarkDecisionsAreSorted` checks the decision itself. With the conjunct, every evaluation already requires the order (`CanEvaluate` reads `IsWellFormed`), so `IsMarkedClosedSorted`'s precondition is the arena's.
