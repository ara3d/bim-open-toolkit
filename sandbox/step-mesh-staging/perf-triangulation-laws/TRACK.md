- `Law_Perf_QualityRequestsOnBoxes` states more than the original's "some face of the 2 x 3 x 4 box asks": a face of that box asks exactly when it is a 2 x 4 face (extent sum 6), the one shape whose two triangles stay below 30 degrees.
