## Findings
- The plan's test column and TKT-396 name `ToolingLawRunnerLaws` and `ToolingDumpsLaws`; neither block exists (layer 9's law blocks are `ToolingDiffsLaws`, `ToolingGeneratorsLaws` and `ToolingReportsLaws`), so no law tested the runner or the dumps before this chunk.
- L1's `Law_WitnessMeshValidatesClean` reads the outcome's whole report, while the runner's `WitnessMeshValidatesCleanAt` checks only `ValidateModelMesh`. The two differ on a witness whose model or loop findings make the outcome not Ok while its mesh validates clean. To keep the rows identical the runner keeps the old claim (`WitnessMeshValidatesCleanOf` reads `MeshChecks`) at no extra cost: `CheckedWitnessShared` keeps the mesh validation it computed for the outcome.
- No witness the library has makes a refinement round within a law's reach: the unit cylinder and the quarter cylinder make none under the witness options at the Structural level, and the sphere, torus, cone, B-spline patch solid and pole-loop sphere each exceed 30 s interpreted before their first state is built. Refinement requests are rare by design (QuadCountMismatch only with PreferQuads; the quality requests are not wired). So `StatesOf` past the first state rests on construction (the same guard and Step as `StateAfter`) and on one-state witnesses (the unit box and the cylinder), not on a refining witness: coverage debt until a cheap refining witness exists.
- The dispatch asked `PipelineWitnessRunsOnce` to read L1's `MeshedWitness` values where they exist. It builds every witness with `CheckedWitnessOf` instead: inside a function a `Witness_*` value is rebuilt at every use on the compiled backend (M3's lesson), so reading `Witness_UnitBoxMeshed` saves nothing, and L1's values do not hold the separate validation reports the check runs and `WitnessMeshValidatesClean` read. It reuses L1's type (inside `CheckedWitness`), L1's planted witness, L1's laws over `MeshedWitness` and `FailedFaceWitness`, and L1's `WitnessMeshUnvalidated`.
- Every compiled run with this folder reports "1 degraded bodies"; the CLI does not name the body. Not investigated.
- A compiled `LawReportOnce` still stalls on the curved witnesses' Oracle validation (L1: the unit cylinder's `MeshModel` did not finish in 10 minutes compiled); it now runs once per witness instead of about 6 + 2E times, but the stall waits on the validation chunks.

## Lessons
- A law comparing a new form with an old one that meshes at Oracle cannot fit 2 s compiled: put both values in one `Witness_*` pair (built once, timed apart from the law), and run the old functions themselves only in an interpreted probe, whose call memo makes them cheap.
- `plato_laws.py` gave "0 laws" for a probe block's name while its law names matched; filter by law names when a block name does not match.
