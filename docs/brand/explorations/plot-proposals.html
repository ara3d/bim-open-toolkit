BIM Open Plot: brand proposals

Drawn 2026-10-11 for the sixth product, ara3d/bim-open-plot, the chart, drawing and figure layer. Three marks from the family's parts (the rows, the wire, the node disc), three accents that no product or status colour uses, each in a tab strip beside the five decided marks. The rules are those of docs/BRANDING.md: 24 by 24 viewBox, one path, one fill.

The three marks

P1 Flow to Bars

The node disc on the left, as in Flow to Page and the viewer mark; one wire leaves it and becomes the first of three rising bars. It says the product is a client of the flow host, and that an answer becomes a picture. Below 16 px the bars alone are the favicon.

explorations/mark-p1-flow-to-bars.svg

P2 Rows to Bars

The three key cells of Model to Rows, each followed by a bar whose length is its value: a table that is already a horizontal bar chart. The most literal of the three and the closest to the Data and Schema marks. Weakness: at 16 px it can pass for a list icon.

explorations/mark-p2-rows-to-bars.svg

P3 Flow to Line

The disc and wire, then the wire itself becomes a line chart over a baseline. Says "plot" as a verb most directly. Weakness: the diagonal is the only one in the family, which is all orthogonal, and it softens at 16 px.

The family of six

Read left to right: rows become a flow; the flow becomes a page, a 3D view, or a plot; a model becomes rows; and the rows have a shape. The three clients of the host (Notebook, Viewer, Plot) all start from the disc on the left.

The chart's series colour is the accent only when a chart has one series; several series take the data palette, which is a vocabulary and not a brand colour, as the node stripes are.

Recommendation

P1 Flow to Bars in ochre, written as docs/brand/plot-mark.svg and docs/brand/plot-lockup.svg.

  • P1 keeps the family grammar: the three clients of the host, Notebook, Viewer and Plot, all start from the disc on the left and differ only in what the wire becomes. P2 has no disc and reads as data rather than as a tool; P3 adds the family's only diagonal.
  • Ochre is the one warm accent in a family that is otherwise cool apart from rose, and it is a traditional drawing colour. It is distinct from the amber status colour in both lightness and saturation (#a16207 beside #d97706 in the strip above), and a chart page will show amber only on a stale embed, in a badge, never on the chrome.
  • Olive is distinct but sits near the green status colour at 16 px; coral is handsome but a chart accent that reads as "error" beside red is a cost every page would pay.
  • Below 16 px the three bars alone are the favicon, as the rules provide for the other marks.
