BIM Open Schema brand proposals

Three marks and two accents for the fifth product, the specification the other four implement or consume. Everything else comes from docs/BRANDING.md: the fonts, the neutrals, the status colours, the two-weight wordmark, and a 24 × 24 single-path mark. Each mark is shown in a five-tab strip with the four decided marks, because the schema will most often appear beside them.

Recommendation

Take mark S1, Table Shape, in slate (#4f5d78), with the lockup BIM Open Schema. The four decided marks are verbs: rows become a flow, a flow becomes a page, a node becomes slabs, a model becomes rows. The schema is the noun they share, so its mark is the rows with nothing flowing in or out: three rows split into a key cell and a value cell, as the data mark draws them, under a solid header bar that gives them the shape of a table. In isolation it is a table icon. In the family it is the root glyph that Flow and Data are drawn from, and that is the point. Slate is the one saturated-enough hue left that is not a product accent, a status colour, or the hub's text black, and a muted accent suits a product whose pages are tables of fields rather than buttons.

  • Mark S2, Hollow Rows, says "shape with no data" most clearly, and loses it at 16 px, where the cut-outs close; the same finding as Block Flow and Ribbon Page.
  • Mark S3, Rows with Brace, reads as a declaration, but the left spine already means "page" in the notebook mark, and one family should not give one stroke two meanings.
  • Below 16 px, S1 uses a header and one split row.
  • Ochre is the other open region. It sits beside the amber status colour, and a spec page that marks fields "pending" would blur the two.

1. The family so far

Decided 2026-10-03: four marks built from the rows, the wire and the node disc; accents blue, violet, teal, rose. Status colours green, amber, red. The toolkit hub uses the text colour. A schema accent has to be none of those.

2. Marks

Mark S1: Table Shape

mark-s1-table-shape.svg, lockup-s1-table-shape.svg (single fill, nonzero)

A solid header bar over three split rows: a short key cell and a long value cell each, in the data mark's proportions. No wire, no node, no motion, because the schema is the thing that is defined, not the thing that happens to it. The header is what carries the mark at small sizes: at 16 px the row gaps thin to hairlines but the heavy bar and the two-column rhythm still read as a table, where three rows alone would be a dotted grid. The weakness is familiarity; on its own it could be any product's table icon. The family answers that: beside the flow mark, which starts from these rows, and the data mark, which ends in them, it reads as the origin.

Mark S2: Hollow Rows

mark-s2-hollow-rows.svg, lockup-s2-hollow-rows.svg (single fill, evenodd)

Three rows drawn as frames, each with a key cell and a value cell cut out of it: the shape of the data with no data inside. It is the most literal picture of a schema and the most different from the four decided marks, which are all solid. At 48 and 96 px it is handsome and reads at once. At 16 px the frames are a one-pixel wall around a one-pixel hole, and the mark becomes a grey block with texture, the failing the flow and notebook proposals both recorded for negative-space marks. It also drops the heavy single shapes the rest of the family relies on, so it looks lighter than its neighbours in a tab strip.

Mark S3: Rows with Brace

mark-s3-rows-with-brace.svg, lockup-s3-rows-with-brace.svg (single fill, nonzero)

The flow mark's three rows, split into key and value, with a vertical bar at the left margin, the way a type declaration or a struct is set in a listing. It says "definition" without needing the family for context, and it holds at 16 px because every element is solid and the bar anchors the silhouette. The problem is the bar itself: in the notebook mark a left spine means a page, and one stroke should not stand for two things in one family. Side by side, Schema and Notebook would look like variants of each other rather than like different products.

4. A specification page in the family type

The schema's pages are tables of fields. Body text uses the notebook's reading scale (14.5 px on a 1.55 line height); the field table uses the interface scale, so a table of columns looks like a table of rows in the studio. Field and type names are Fira Code. The badge is the soft tint.
