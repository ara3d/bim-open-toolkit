Serves W3 (get the answer in every form and hand it to someone). The owner wants a notebook another person can open in their local notebook environment or in an online session. The notebook keeps a snapshot of every result, so the public page can show it with no host; Re-evaluate needs a host that holds the model. Scope lists a hosted multi-user service as Out. Options: 1. Online means the public page with snapshots only; Re-evaluate is local. 2. A read-only hosted host over the public sample models, no accounts. 3. A hosted host that accepts an uploaded model, which reopens the Out line. Default: option 1.
